# Daerlieth Nightstar tracker: build brief

This folder is a working single-file tracker (`index.html`) for a Pathfinder 1e gestalt character: Eternal Mage 8 (Artillery Expert) / Spell-less Ranger 8, mythic Archmage tier 2. The player is the only editor. Her GM only needs to view.

## Status
The hosted version is built: Firebase (Firestore document `trackers/{characterId}`, security rules in `firestore.rules`), email/password sign-in for the player, public live view for the GM, PWA with offline edits that sync on reconnect. Setup steps are in `README.md`.

## Goal
Turn it into a hosted web app:
1. Public read-only link for the GM. No account needed to view.
2. Editing locked to the player (login, or a secret edit link).
3. Live shared state, so the GM sees changes as she makes them.
4. Installable on a phone (PWA, works offline for the editor, syncs when back online).
Keep the look (black background, silver-blue accent, Marcellus / Albert Sans / IBM Plex Mono) and every rule below.

## What exists
- `levels.js`: level progression. `Levels.prog(L)` gives every level-dependent number (BAB and iteratives, gestalt saves = best side, caster level, spells known and highest spell level, arcane buffer, overdrive pool/ignore, concentration slots, artillery dice and spell level, secret DC, stealth attack, forest bonus, fast movement, unarmed die, experimental spell level, and Gwathfaron's companion-table row). `Levels.choices(L)` lists her own picks (feats incl. the GM's extra feat at odd levels, ability increases, secrets, schools, talents, combat style, Enhanced Companion, favored enemies, spells known, Gwathfaron's feats/increases/tricks); `Levels.gains(L)` feeds the level-up dialog. Never hard-code level 8 numbers elsewhere.
- Tabs: **Daerlieth** (the tracker panels on top: clock bar, one resources panel for burnout, overdrive and mythic power, each with a "How it works" button (`data-help` burn / od / myth: `burnHelpHtml`, `odHelpHtml` with everything overdrive pays for, `mythHelpHtml` with everything mythic power pays for plus the free mythic abilities and later tiers), spells, cast, concentration, hit points; her sheet below; the log at the bottom) and **Gwathfaron** (`#gwathfaron`). `setView` toggles `body.view-pet`, which hides the tracker panels. There is no separate Tracker tab.
- Spells panel (full width, above Concentration): three pickers side by side, Known spells (`#knownSel`, `ui.knownSel`: her list plus level-up picks), Experimental (`#expSel`) and Wild Arcana (`#waSel`). All three start on "Choose a spell…" with the block below collapsed; the experimental and Wild Arcana lists leave out spells she already knows (`knownNames`). Under each is its own cast block (`castHtml(sp, col)`, col `k`/`x`/`w`): overdrive, mythic and Cast on options (Wild Arcana only in its own column, whose list leaves out spells slower than a standard action), burnout, CL, DC, damage and a Cast button. Each column keeps its own options in `ui.cols` (`withCol`), and control ids carry the column suffix (`odChk-k`...). There is no separate cast panel; the GM view shows the blocks without options or Cast buttons.
- `sheet.js`: her sheet (under the tracker panels on the Daerlieth tab). `SheetEngine.derive(state)` computes every sheet number (abilities, AC, saves, attacks, DCs, skills, load) from the tracker state plus `state.eq` (gear off when `eq[key] === false`; the Gear worn panel labels each item with its slot (`ITEMS[].slot`) and lists the open slots, freeing a slot when its item is set aside) and `state.cond` (conditions). `SheetEngine.render` builds the markup. Its collapsible sections (`sec`) all start closed; `renderSheet` keeps the ones she opens open across re-renders. The tracker also calls `derive` for Cha, touch bonus, AC and the overdrive pool, so worn gear changes both tabs. Edit sheet numbers here, not in `index.html`.
- Portrait: `images/daerlieth.webp` (full) and `images/daerlieth-thumb.webp` (240 px head-and-shoulders crop). The round thumbnail (`[data-portrait]`, `.portrait`) sits beside her name in the headline panel (portrait, name, level buttons, key numbers: `render(st, {headOnly: true})`, drawn into `#sheetHead` at the top of `main`, above the tracker panels; the sheet below leaves it out); tapping it opens the full picture in the info dialog (works read-only). Gwathfaron's are `images/gwathfaron.webp` and `images/gwathfaron-thumb.webp` (head crop), in the same place at the top of his tab (`data-portrait="gwathfaron"`). All four are in the service worker's shell.
- Gwathfaron tab (`#gwathfaron`): `SheetEngine.renderPet`, built from `PET` and `derivePet` in `sheet.js` (small cat companion at effective druid level 8 with Improved Animal Companion; all-round scout build: Int 3, Skill Focus (Stealth), Skill Focus (Perception), Cunning (+1 rank per Hit Die: `p.cunning`, `ranksMax`), Alertness; 14 ranks: Stealth 4, Perception 3, Survival 2, Escape Artist 1, Intimidate 1, Climb 1, Swim 1, Acrobatics 1). Renders into `#sheetView` like the Sheet tab.
- Feats, traits and talents: `featsSec` in `sheet.js` (her sheet, replacing the printed sheet's feat paragraph; Gwathfaron's Feats section uses it too). Each entry is a `featBtn`: hover shows a one-line summary (`.tip`), tap opens the full text in the info dialog (`featHtml`, `[data-feat]` listener, works read-only). Descriptions live in `FEAT_INFO` (keyed by lower-case name; includes likely future picks such as Outflank and Precise Strike). Her third trait is Gifted Adept (scorching ray +1 CL: `gifted` in `compute`, `clRay`/`rays` in `derive`), taken for the Umbral Unmasking drawback. Her 5th-level feats are Outflank and Precise Strike (`BASE_FEATS`; `hasFeat` counts them): flanking is +4 for both (`flankBonus`) and melee hits add +1d6 precision while flanking (`precise`), on her sheet and Gwathfaron's. Later level-up feat, talent, combat style and Enhanced Companion picks appear in place, or as open slots.
- Every rules item on both sheets uses the same info button (`featBtn(key, label, extra)` / `featHtml`; hover = summary plus her numbers, tap = full text): skills (`skill:` keys; both skills tables have a Take 10/20 column and each skill pop-up gives the take 10 and take 20 rules: `takes`, `takeCell`, `takeText`, `TAKE_RULES` in `sheet.js`; Use Magic Device allows neither, and skills with no retry (Knowledge, Appraise, Sense Motive, Spellcraft) show -- for take 20), Gwathfaron's tricks (`trick:`) and special abilities (`pet:`) from `rules-info.js` (`window.RulesInfo`), and her Spell-less Ranger, Eternal Mage and mythic abilities from `classSecs` in `sheet.js` (`ab:` keys, rebuilt each render so their numbers follow her level; these sections replace the printed sheet's ranger, mage and mythic prose).
- RPoL export: a "Generate RPoL sheet" button at the bottom of each sheet (`[data-rpol]`, `rpolOpen` in `index.html`) opens a dialog with the sheet as plain fixed-width text, Copy and Download. `rpol.js` (`RpolSheet.her` / `.pet`) builds it from `SheetEngine.derive` on the base state (no toggles, active spells, burnout or Strength damage), `featGroups`, `classData` and the castable spells, and copies the static parts (personal details, gear, magic items, description, background) from `docs/daerlieth-sheet.txt` / `docs/gwathfaron-sheet.txt`, so keep those printed sheets' section headings. Beyond the base sheet it adds what the tracker knows now: flanking with other allies, notes for long arm, blood money, detonate and mythic spells, untrained Knowledge and take 10/20, worn slots and open slots, what overdrive and mythic power pay for, later tricks for Gwathfaron (`ptrick-*` picks, also on his tab), his hasted full attack, and a CURRENT STATUS section on each sheet (`nowLines`: HP, burnout and CL now, overdrive, mythic, light, vials, Strength damage, concentration, gear set aside, status effects, conditions).
- This round (`#pTurn`, `renderTurn`): action economy for her and Gwathfaron. `state.turn = {her: [], pet: []}`, entries `{id, act, name, note, grants}` with act std/move/swift/imm/free/react/full/step/none/long (react = attack of opportunity on an enemy's turn: uses none of her actions, one a round, more with Combat Reflexes). `actions.js` (`Actions.catalog` builds the "Add an action" list from the live sheet: attacks, spells with their casting times from the codex, mythic, movement, every usable skill with its usual action (`SKILL_ACT`, also shown in skill pop-ups), Gwathfaron's attacks and tricks; `Actions.budget` shows what's left and warns when over; `Actions.fits` hides from the list what she has no action left for: after a standard only move and smaller, after a move or full-round only swift, 5-ft step and free; actions that move her (`moves`) rule out the 5-ft step and vice versa; "Show all" brings them back). Casts, Blood Money, Surge and Amazing Initiative add themselves (`turnAdd`). `oocText` builds the OOC line (actions, effects, conditions, HP and resources); End round saves it as `state.prevOoc` (Last round) and clears the turn.
- Letters of travel: `LETTERS` in `sheet.js` (Kibrat Erbetsim, five Morningglory Kingdom regions, Eniran, Ekuran, Serasole), a section after Gear and money on her sheet; picking one from the dropdown opens it in the info dialog (`letterHtml`, change listener on `select[data-letter]`, works read-only).
- `sheet-prose.js`: generated static text for the Sheet tab. Rebuild with `python3 tools/make-prose.py <printed sheet>.txt > sheet-prose.js`.
- `sheet.css`: styles for the tabs and the Sheet.
- `index.html`: all HTML, CSS and JS in one file. The log (`log`, `LOG_MAX` 500, newest first) is the long record for retcons past the 20-round rollback: casts, recoveries, expiries, every manual adjustment (`logAdj` merges repeated taps on the same value in a round into one "HP 82 → 68" line), conditions, gear, status effects and a "Round N actions" line at each End round; Copy log (also in the GM view) copies it oldest first, and Clear log needs two taps. Keeps a local copy in `localStorage` key `ilmadria-burnout-ledger-v1` (plus an `:edit` flag).
- Storage is isolated in the storage section at the bottom of the script (`fitPayload` keeps a save under 180 KB, below the 200 KB the rules allow, by dropping the oldest round snapshots, then the oldest log lines; `initStore`, `applyRemote`, `setUser`, `scheduleSave`, `flush`, `setReadOnly`, `setEditable`). It talks to Firebase using `config.js`; with an empty config it falls back to local-only. Everything else is pure UI and rules.
- `.ro` class on `<body>` hides every editing control (read-only mode already exists; reuse it for the GM view). `?view` (`VIEW_ONLY`) forces the GM view even on a device where the editor is signed in, and hides the account buttons.
- `data/codex.json`: every Pathfinder 1e spell for every class (3,040, from The Spell Codex spreadsheet): school, levels by class, casting time, components, range, area/effect/targets, duration, save, SR, source, full text and mythic text. Loaded on demand (`loadCodex`, `codexFind`) and cached by the service worker. The "Spell text" / "i" buttons open it (`spellCard`), and the status-effect form's Spell or effect box is a dropdown of every spell someone could cast on her or Gwathfaron (personal-range spells left out; `fxSugShow` in `index.html`, `.fxsug`), narrowed as she types (names starting with the text, then word starts, then anywhere; lasting spells before instantaneous), showing classes and levels, school and duration; picking one fills in type, duration (from the caster level field) and a note (`fxAutofill`).
- `data/spell-bank.json`: the wizard list, levels 1 to 9 (1,862 spells) with school, duration code and flags (F fire, L casting time over a standard action), inlined in `index.html` as `BANK` for the experimental spell and Wild Arcana pickers (`#expSel`, `#waSel`: both list the bank by level up to what she can cast). Both files are rebuilt with `python3 -I tools/make-codex.py <The_Spell_Codex.xlsx>`, which also rewrites the inline `BANK`.

## State (one JSON document)
`v, round, level, burnout, gained, overdrive, mythic, lightUsed, hp, petHp, eq, cond, fx, picks, hpRolls, ranks, petRanks, vials, strDmg, blood, exp, expLocked, turn, prevOoc, undo[], active[], pending[], log[]`
- `active[]`: `{id, name, cast, end (null = open-ended), total, on}`, max 2 entries. `on` is who the spell is on: `her` (default), `pet` or `ally` (Cast on, in the cast panel). Only spells on her change her sheet numbers, except haste (`hastedOn` in `sheet.js`, matched by name so any way she casts it counts, or a Haste status effect from someone else): on her it covers Gwathfaron too (one creature per level), on `pet` only him. Haste gives +1 attack, +1 dodge AC, +1 Reflex, +30 ft speed and one extra strike at the highest bonus on a full attack, on both sheets and in the This round list.
- `pending[]`: Detonate reminders `{id, name, fire, text}`.
- `cond.atk` (`std` | `charge` | `aoo`, absent = full attack) and `cond.hands` (`one`, absent = two weapons): the attack picker on her tab; the Attacks table shows only that combination.
- `cond.flank` (hers) and `cond.petFlank` (Gwathfaron's): who they flank with, since a teamwork feat counts only when the partner has it too: absent = not flanking, `plain` (+2), `out` (an Outflank ally, +4), `prec` (a Precise Strike ally, +2 and +1d6 precision), `both` (her with Gwathfaron or him with her: +4 and +1d6; picking it on one sets the other). Gwathfaron's picker offers only not flanking, `plain` and `both`: Coordinated Companion gives him her teamwork feats only with her (an old `out`/`prec` reads as `plain`). An old `true` reads as `both`. `flankMode`, `flankAtk`, `flankPrec`, `flankSeg` in `sheet.js`. Any flank turns on her stealth attack, `cond.art` (`sniper` | `burst`, absent = touch) and `cond.artD8` (artillery picker on her tab), `cond.petAtk` (`std` | `charge` | `aoo`, absent = full attack; Gwathfaron's picker).
- `fx`: status effects `{her: [], pet: []}`, each `{id, name, from, kind: good|bad, left (rounds, null = until removed), note}`. Entered in each sheet's Status effects panel and listed (with End) under Concentration; reminders only; `left` counts down each round and the effect drops at 0; times show as hours, minutes and rounds (`fxLeft`). Pass time (1 min / 10 min / 1 hour, under Concentration) advances that many rounds at once (`passTime`): effects and spells count down and burnout recovers 1 per round.
- `level` (8 to 20, default 8): set by Level up / Undo on her tab. `hpRolls` {level: d10 roll} for levels above 8 (HP = 82 + roll + Con each). `picks` {choice id: text or ability key}: her level-up picks; `asi-*` and `pasi-*` (ability increases) and `fep-*` (+2 to a favored enemy) change numbers, a feat named Greater Two-Weapon Fighting adds its strike, spells known (`sp-<level>-<spell level>-<n>`) search that level's wizard spells (`#wizL0`..`#wizL9` datalists, built from the codex) and join her castable list (`knownSpells`), the rest are records. `ranks` / `petRanks`: skill ranks once new ones are placed (rank buttons show only while ranks are unplaced).
- `exp`: experimental spell id (empty = no pick yet, a preset id such as `haste`, or a `b-...` bank id), up to `Levels.prog(L).expMax`. `expLocked`: the pick is locked in for the day ("Lock in" button in the experimental column, which has no Cast button). Once locked the picker is disabled, and the spell joins the Known spells list (`castable()`, `lockedExp()`), and is left out of Wild Arcana. New day unlocks it (the pick stays selected).

## Rules the tracker encodes
- Character: caster level 8, Cha 22 (+6), concentration +14.
- **Eldritch burnout.** A cast of 1st level or higher gains `spell level - 2` (arcane buffer, minimum 0), +1 if it is the experimental spell. Orisons gain none. Arcane artillery counts as 4th level (2). Each point is -1 CL. Every 2 points is -1 DC and -1d6 artillery damage. At the end of any round with no burnout gained, recover 1. At CL 0 only orisons can be cast. "End round" (the old "No cast") ends the round: durations count down and burnout recovers 1 if none was gained that round. Casting no longer ends the round.
- **Arcane overdrive**, pool 10. Spend 1 (free action) to ignore up to 4 burnout from one cast. Artillery overdrive is a separate 1-point spend (d6 to d8). Refills after 8 hours of sleep ("New day").
- **Limited concentration.** 2 durational spells at once. A third ends the oldest. Detonate holds a slot until it goes off.
- **Experimental spell.** One pick per day from the whole sorcerer/wizard list (any school, focus or not), any level below her highest castable (so up to 3rd at 8th level), +1 burnout. At 11th level it may be her highest level for +1 more burnout; at 17th she gets a second one.
- **Mythic.** 7 power per day. Wild Arcana costs 1, is a standard action (per the Mythic Adventures FAQ), casts any spell on her list, known or not, with casting time 1 standard action or less, and gives +2 CL. Elemental Bond (fire) gives +2 CL to fire-descriptor spells and fire resistance 10. Eldritch Breach rolls caster level checks twice.
- **Blood Money** is a swift action: 1d6 damage, and blood replaces one material component this round. Components over 1 gp cost 1 Strength damage plus 1 per full 500 gp.
- **Detonate**: costs 50 gp in vials, max 10d8, holds a concentration slot for 1 round.
- Top bar: the round number framed with ROUND under it, a + button (a round with no actions: same as End round, logged as "passed, no actions") with a − under it (undo the last round, so the GM can retcon a fight: End round and + push a snapshot of the whole play state (`roundSnap`: everything except log, undo and her build: level, picks, hpRolls, ranks, petRanks) to `state.undo`, last 20 (`UNDO_MAX`); Reset, New day and Pass time clear it), Reset, End round, New day (spells are cast from the Spells panel only). Reset clears concentration. Reset and New day both turn off favored enemy (`cond.target`) and favored terrain (`cond.forest`) (`clearScene`). New day needs two taps.
- **Hit points.** Daerlieth 82 max; Hard to Kill: stabilizes below 0, dies at -32 (twice Con 16). Gwathfaron (panther companion) 50 max, dies at -15. New day heals 8 and 7 (natural healing).
- **Combat Reflexes** (her 6th-level Unarmed style feat; it replaced Stunning Fist, which is gone from the tracker): 1 + Dex attacks of opportunity a round (7), even flat-footed (`d.atk.aoo`, also the This round Reaction cap). **Long arm** (replaced feather fall as a 1st-level spell known): while active on her, reach 10 ft (`d.atk.reach`). **Mythic Surge** (+1d6 to a d20) and **Amazing Initiative** (extra standard action, no spells or artillery) each cost 1 mythic power.
- **Mythic spells** (Mythic Spell Lore): fireball (d10s, burning 2d6/round on a failed save) and dimension door (one-way portal for up to CL allies), +1 mythic power each.
- Ranged touch +14 (BAB 8 + Dex 6). Dimension door carries 1 willing Medium or smaller creature per 3 CL (2 at CL 8).
- Strength buttons act on the score: "Strength -1" adds Strength damage, "+1" heals it.

## Open rulings for the GM (no longer shown on the page)
Whether Detonate counts as a fire spell, whether cantrips with durations use a concentration slot, whether Wild Arcana may use spells outside her focus schools (Evocation, Abjuration, Transmutation, plus Conjuration proposed), and whether Blood Money's blood replaces both Detonate vials.

## Suggested stack
Static hosting plus a small realtime store (Firebase, Supabase or Cloudflare Durable Objects). One document per character. Writes require auth; reads are public. Keep `index.html` working without a build step if you can.
