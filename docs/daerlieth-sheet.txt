DESCRIPTION
Daerlieth is tall and lean even for an elf, with the long-fingered, quick hands of a lifelong skirmisher. Ivory-white skin with blue tints, silver-white hair with a faint blue sheen worn in a loose braid, blue eyes with gold flecks. She dresses for the road in a mithral chain shirt under a hooded moon-gray cloak, with her family's eight-pointed silver star on a simple pin. She never looks hurried, but her eyes are always moving, tracking the tree line, the exits and the people who aren't talking. No staff, wand or spellbook, which unsettles Cormanthyr's mages.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------
BACKGROUND
House Nightstar (Teu'Tel'Quessir). Moon elf of a minor line. At twenty she cast her first spell untaught, and her magic has never needed a spellbook, preparation or prayer. Her aunt Gweyr, the house archmage, found no discipline in it, and the wizards called her lazy. She left for the border woods and spent decades with the green elf scouts, learning to track, fight bare-handed and live off the land, then returned to the City of Song as a ranger who can hurl raw arcane force. Her grandfather Seith, advisor to the Harpers at Twilight, recommended her for the Coronal's Defenders. Panther companion Gwathfaron (Sindarin, "shadow hunter"); scout mentor: name not yet chosen.
